# Phase 2 Research Programme — Proposed, Not Completed

**Status:** planning only. No Phase 2 experiment, hardware allocation or
resource consumption has occurred for this release. The programme is
conditional on passing the Phase 2 entry gates and on owner authorisation.

**Programme:** 30 November 2026–22 January 2027; final pitch planned for
25 January 2027. One-person team; staged and resource-bounded research.

| Stage | Route A (primary) | Route B (conditional after M1) | Shared reference |
|---|---|---|---|
| M1 | Reversible multi-year contract and register design | Factor-level construction / resource feasibility contract | Exact reference, accuracy and stop criteria |
| M2 | Bounded multi-year accumulator or event predicate | Factor-level prototype if admitted | Independent correctness and resources |
| M3 | Adjoint, clean-ancilla and Grover component validation | Bond rank, contraction and error scaling | Resource growth evidence |
| M4 | Matched full-cost comparison | Exact-DP / VE and TN same-task comparison | Matched accuracy, elapsed time, memory |
| M5 | Freeze evidence and report limitations | Freeze positive and negative findings | Pitch and GO/NO-GO recommendation |

**Expected deliverables:** (A) bounded reversible quantum risk component,
(B) conditional factor-level TN feasibility evidence, and (C) strong-classical
comparative benchmark. An optional cross-route feasibility assessment may
explore whether measured TN structure informs quantum state preparation in
M3–M4, only if both routes pass their individual gates.

**Proposed computing budget (not allocated hardware):** Route A 239,
Route B 101 and shared 40 CPU core-hours (380 in total); optional GPU hours
Route A 25 and Route B 35 (up to 60 total). CPU core-hours are not human
labour-hours or promised hardware access.

A complete three-year five-metric quantum solver, finite-shot QAE/IQAE, and
physical hardware experiments are not guaranteed Phase 2 deliverables.
